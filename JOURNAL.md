# June 5: Finished Electronics Mounting
Today I finished the electronics casing and the magnetic attachments to snap on to the stand. Originally I also wanted to be able to take it out and snap it on to my t-shirt to wear as a cool accessory, but then I realised I would need 2 seperate microcontrollers: one for the stand and another for the reactor itself, and they would have to sync together, and I reallly didn't want to code all that, so I got rid of the t-shirt aspect. Maybe in the future, I'll make an arc reactor only meant to be worn on my shirt.
